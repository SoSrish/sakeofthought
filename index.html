    How it works

    1. Build your cart

      Choose shapes, what to paint, and how many.

    2. Send it on WhatsApp

      Proceed to buy opens WhatsApp with your full order written out. Share your reference photos in the same chat.

    3. Confirm and pay in the chat

      We confirm the design, shipping and total, then share payment details there. Orders are prepaid.

    4. We paint and ship

      Your order reaches you in 10 to 12 days, 15 at most, anywhere in India.

    Questions

    How long does an order take?

    10 to 12 days from when we confirm your order, 15 at most. Need it for a date? Add it under "Needed by" in your cart.

    Do you deliver to my city?

    We ship across India. Shipping cost depends on your order and pincode, and we confirm it in the WhatsApp chat.

    How do I pay?

    Orders are prepaid. Once we confirm the design, shipping and total on WhatsApp, we share the payment details there.

    Can I cancel my order?

    Every piece is made for you, so a confirmed order can't be cancelled.

    What if it arrives broken?

    Send us a video showing the damage and we will refund it.

    What photo should I send for the caricature?

    A clear photo where the face is well lit and easy to see works best. Send it in the WhatsApp chat after you place your order.

    Can I order something that isn't in the catalogue?

    Yes. Not covered what you want? Just let us know and we'll make it happen. Tap "Request something custom" or message us on WhatsApp.

    Can you put a QR code on a tote?

    Yes. Tell us what it should open (a song, a photo album, a voice note) and send the link in the chat. We make the QR for you.

    I have a coupon or referral code

    Type it in your cart before you tap "Proceed to buy". We confirm it in the WhatsApp chat.

    Your privacy

    • Your cart stays on your own phone or computer until you send it. Nothing reaches us before you press send in WhatsApp.
    • We use your name, number, address and reference photos only to make and deliver your order. We share them with no one except the courier.
    • We keep a basic record of each order for our accounts, and delete reference photos once the order is delivered.
    • We post a finished piece on Instagram only if you say yes.
    • Want your details removed? Message us on WhatsApp and we will delete them.
