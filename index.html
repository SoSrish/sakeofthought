    How it works

    1. Build your cart

      Choose shapes, what to paint, and how many.

    2. Send it on WhatsApp

      Proceed to buy opens WhatsApp with your full order written out. Share your reference photos in the same chat.

    3. Confirm and pay in the chat

      We confirm the design, shipping and total, then share payment details there. Orders are prepaid.

    4. We paint and ship

      Your order reaches you in 10 to 12 days, 15 at most, anywhere in India.

    Good to know

    • Shipping. We ship across India. The cost depends on your order and is confirmed in the chat.
    • Time. 10 to 12 days from confirmation, 15 at most.
    • Payment. Orders are prepaid. Payment details are shared in the WhatsApp chat.
    • Cancellations. Every piece is made for you, so a confirmed order can't be cancelled.
    • Breakage. If a piece arrives broken, send us a video showing the damage and we will refund it.

    Your privacy

    • Your cart stays on your own phone or computer until you send it. Nothing reaches us before you press send in WhatsApp.
    • We use your name, number, address and reference photos only to make and deliver your order. We share them with no one except the courier.
    • We keep a basic record of each order for our accounts, and delete reference photos once the order is delivered.
    • We post a finished piece on Instagram only if you say yes.
    • Want your details removed? Message us on WhatsApp and we will delete them.
